We ended 2024 with full-year revenue of $88.3 million, up 22% from $72.4 million in 2023.

EBITDA came in at $19.4 million — that's a margin of 22.0%, up from 18.6% in 2023. We said we'd hit 21% and we beat it.

TTM Revenue as of December 31, 2024: $88.3M
TTM EBITDA: $19.4M
Gross Margin: 54.2%

On the balance sheet, net debt at year-end is $31.7 million, which puts us at 1.6x levered — the lowest since we closed the deal.

Current headcount is 634, up from 581 at the start of the year.

[Slide 3 — Entry vs. Current]

To frame the value created — when you acquired us in March 2021, the business was doing $51M in revenue and $9.2M EBITDA at a 12.1% margin. The entry EV/EBITDA was 8.0x and we carried $38M in net debt at close.

Entry Revenue: $51M
Entry EBITDA: $9.2M
Entry Gross Margin: 44%
Entry Net Debt: $38M
Entry EV Multiple: 8.0x
Entry Headcount: 498

Today, we've more than doubled EBITDA, expanded margins by 10 points, and reduced leverage by 40%.

[Slide 4 — How We Got Here]

Three initiatives drove this:

1. Care Coordination Platform — launched Q2 2022, now covers 78% of patient touchpoints. Reduced readmission rates by 14%, directly improving CMS quality scores and reimbursement rates.

2. Geographic Expansion — entered two new MSAs (Phoenix and Nashville). These markets contributed $11.2M in 2024 revenue, tracking toward $18M in 2025.

3. Payor Mix Optimization — commercial insurance mix improved from 38% to 51% of revenue over three years. That's the primary driver of gross margin expansion.

[Slide 5 — 2025 Priorities]

Our three priorities for 2025:
  - Hit $105M revenue and $22M+ EBITDA
  - Complete the RCM (revenue cycle management) tech migration by Q2
  - Evaluate two potential bolt-on targets in the Southeast (LOIs pending)
