INVESTMENT THESIS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. PREMIUMIZATION TAILWIND — Mass-to-premium trade-up in personal care has driven 8–12% category CAGR over 5 years. Keystone is positioned in the $25–$60 price tier, the fastest-growing segment.

2. DTC ACCELERATION — Online revenue grew from 18% to 31% of mix over 3 years. Each DTC point of shift adds ~150bps to gross margin. Target: 42% DTC by Year 3.

3. OPERATIONAL IMPROVEMENT — Supply chain consolidation and SKU rationalization (from 240 to ~160 active SKUs) expected to add 200–300bps EBITDA margin in Years 1–2.

4. INTERNATIONAL OPTIONALITY — No meaningful international revenue today. UK and DACH markets are logical entry points given distribution network of strategic partner (Luxe Group).
